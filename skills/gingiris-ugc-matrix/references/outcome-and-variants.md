# Creator outcomes and variant tests / 创作者目标与素材实验

Select the outcome before pricing a collaboration. Keep a production fee for agreed work where appropriate; do not assign all unmeasurable sales risk to the creator.

| Goal | Candidate incentive | Required measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Delivery fee + optional qualified reach bonus | Platform, window, valid views and duplication limits |
| Activation | Fee + verified first-value event bonus | User deduplication, event definition and attribution window |
| Paid demand | Fee + attributed sales share | Actual payment, refunds, attribution conflicts, cap and payout date |

An example fee or bonus is not a market benchmark. Commission is only one part of CAC; include production, distribution and relevant labor. Agree usage rights, paid reuse, platforms and revisions; reuse on another platform is not automatically free. Do not require positive testimonials.

For an original creator, brief audience, task, verified product facts, goal, rights and constraints, leaving room to explore. For execution of a validated format, supply an authorized sample, required shots and acceptance conditions. Do not require undisclosed imitation or copy another creator's identity.

## Same-project variant card

Project and actual user value; A/B hypothesis; one main variable; comparable format/landing page; IDs/UTMs; planned window; cash/time cap; downstream event and quality condition.

Report exposure and click/event counts plus rates. Track natural and paid distribution separately. Recommender allocation differs across posts and is not a randomized A/B test. Few clicks can be inconclusive; showing two variants or observing a competitor repeat a format does not establish a winner or their internal testing sequence.

Continue when the selected business outcome improves within the cost limit without degrading downstream quality. If clicks rise but task completion falls, inspect promise/page mismatch before scaling. At the cap with insufficient evidence, report inconclusive rather than inventing a winning format.
