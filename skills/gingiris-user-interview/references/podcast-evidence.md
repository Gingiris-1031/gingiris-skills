# Podcast evidence for interviews

- An early AFFiNE interview lasted about two hours and later produced a detailed illustrated review. The lesson is depth and trust, not a duration quota.
- Ask the user to screen-share the real workflow; abstract opinions are weak evidence.
- Enthusiasm does not equal paid-user fit. Tag use case, urgency, alternative and paid behavior.
- Close the loop with product and engineering, verify after release and notify the user.
- Claims that other companies ran 1,000 interviews are secondary recollections, not verified primary facts.
