# Iris Product Hunt field notes

Internal source synthesis from the Product Hunt experience wiki supplied by Iris (through 2025-05-31).

## Confirmed case record

- 30 daily #1 launches, 10 weekly top-three launches, and 2 monthly top-five launches, including one monthly #1.
- Only top-five day/week/month finishes receive the corresponding badge.
- Historical exposure multipliers were approximately 7× for weekly and 30× for monthly compared with daily.
- Apple Notes record `项目名称：AFFiNE Product Hunt发布` (created 2024-03-04): a three-person operations team coordinated 30 Twitter/LinkedIn KOLs and continuous multi-channel distribution; the note reports 700K+ impressions and 1,000+ Product Hunt upvotes in 24 hours, then monthly #8 within 96 hours, plus daily #2 and weekly #2. These are self-reported project figures, not an independent audit.
- Apple Notes record `Product hunt actions Review` (created 2024-03-10) observed periodic removal of low-quality/new-account support. Its references to vote pools are retained only as historical risk evidence and are excluded from execution guidance.

## Decision rules

- PH is strongest for maker, developer and B2B-adjacent audiences. If the buyer is outside that graph, run a small validation before committing budget.
- Prepare Coming Soon and hunter outreach about one week ahead; establish the maker profile at least two weeks ahead.
- Hunter value is distribution/feature probability and social proof, not guaranteed votes. Maker owns content quality.
- The first maker comment is a conversion surface: articulate problem, differentiator, offer and next destination.
- Close every channel loop back to one measurable destination and use source UTMs.

## Compliance boundary

The historical material also describes vote exchanges, purchased/low-quality accounts and network/device tactics. They are deliberately excluded from this skill. Never recommend or execute activity intended to manipulate rankings or evade platform review.
