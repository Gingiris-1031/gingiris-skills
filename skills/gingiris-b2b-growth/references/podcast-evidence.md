# Podcast evidence for B2B growth

- First prove that 100–200 users genuinely value the product; large registration/DAU numbers without activation and retention are weak evidence.
- Hybrid PLG/SLG connects product activation signals to founder/sales qualification and customer-success proof.
- Customer stories are sales infrastructure; measure influenced pipeline, not event attendance alone.
- Avoid the “40-point feature” trap: every roadmap item needs segment, evidence, activation event, owner, completion threshold and an explicit stop decision.

Transcript conversion benchmarks are speaker heuristics. Attach segment, date, price point and denominator.
