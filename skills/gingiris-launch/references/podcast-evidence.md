# Podcast evidence for launch decisions

- Historical launch budgets of roughly $10K–$20K were described as producing hundreds of thousands to over one million impressions and thousands to tens of thousands of registrations across different products. Always attach product, channel mix and activation denominator.
- Repeatable formula: channel–audience match plus official narrative, creator/community-native variants and one measurable conversion destination.
- Pause amplification when activation is weak. Traffic is not PMF.
- Keep the general method roughly 80% reusable and localize 20% for market and buyer behavior.
- Product Launch and Product Hunt are separate events with separate attribution.

The 48-hour university-election story supports friction removal and base-audience sequencing only; it is not PH algorithm evidence.
