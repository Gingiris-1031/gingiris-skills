## Voice hypothesis / 文风假设

| Dimension / 维度 | Observed pattern / 观察 | Evidence IDs / 来源 | Counterexample / 反例 | Scope / 适用场景 | Confirmed or provisional / 状态 |

Dimensions: entry, rhythm, vocabulary, certainty, emotion, asides, reader relationship, endings. Avoid reducing the author to one mood or one platform.

维度：开头、节奏、用词、确定程度、情绪、旁白、读者关系、收尾。不要用一种情绪或一个平台定义整个人。

## Guide template / 指南模板

- Priority and version / 优先级与版本
- Stable traits with evidence / 有证据的稳定特征
- Variations by genre and audience / 体裁与受众变化
- Misreadings and counterexamples / 易误读之处和反例
- Facts that require separate verification / 必须单独核实的事实
- Provisional rules / 待确认规则
- Short trial and author corrections / 短试稿与本人修改

## Synthetic calibration example / 教学样例

Original: “今天看了三份反馈。我还没想好要不要改首页，先把那两个看不懂按钮的人约来聊聊。”
Bad adaptation: “在变化的时代，用户洞察是增长的核心。我们必须立即重塑品牌。”
Faithful English adaptation: “I read three pieces of feedback today. I'm not sure the homepage needs changing yet. I'll first talk to the two people who couldn't figure out the button.”

This is invented teaching material, not Iris's experience or quote. The bad version changes uncertainty into certainty, adds a brand conclusion and loses the specific next action. A style match cannot compensate for these meaning changes.

以上是虚构教学例，不是 Iris 的经历或引语。错误稿把不确定变确定、补出品牌结论、丢失具体动作；语言漂亮也不能弥补原意变更。

## Evaluation / 验收

Check: invented facts? changed certainty or endorsement? misattributed quotes? forced format? preserved meaningful repetition and emotional range? natural target-language rhythm? author feedback obtained? Report observations, not a fictitious similarity percentage.

核对：是否造事实、改确定程度/推荐力度、错归引语、强套形式、删掉有意义的重复与情绪？目标语言是否自然？是否得到本人反馈？报告具体差异，不捏造“相似度98%”。
