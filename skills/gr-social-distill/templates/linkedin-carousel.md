# LinkedIn post or carousel

Choose post vs carousel from the user's request, audience and content structure. A consumer product can still address professional buyers, partners or creators here.

For a post: concrete reader problem → supported insight/example → limitation or tradeoff → useful next step.
For a carousel: cover promise → the necessary argument in coherent slides → takeaway/source slide. Use as many slides as the material needs, without padding to a fixed count. Supply slide text and caption; call it a storyboard until an actual document/image artifact exists and is checked.

Use “I/we achieved” only for the author's verified experience. A third-party result remains attributed. A before/after result is optional, never fabricated to fill a slide. Keep text legible and check current document/upload constraints when publication is requested.

Deliver copy, source notes, optional storyboard, and pending checks. Do not publish or schedule by default.
