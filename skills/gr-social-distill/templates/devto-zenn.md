# DEV / Zenn adaptation

Prepare a draft, not a published article. Verify ownership/reuse rights and keep source attribution. Do not turn a third-party account into a personal story.

## Canonical decision

For duplicate or very similar republication, identify the preferred original URL and configure canonical accordingly. A materially distinct article can use its own canonical. Do not force self-canonical to chase a second ranking, or claim a seven-day delay prevents duplication. Canonical is a signal, not a ranking guarantee.

Primary references checked 2026-11-08:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls
- https://dev.to/p/editor_guide

## DEV draft

```yaml
---
title: "[source-backed title]"
published: false
description: "[what the reader will learn]"
tags: [up to four relevant tags]
# canonical_url: [preferred original, if applicable; omit until resolved]
---
```

Write a useful opening, explanation, source-backed examples, limitations and next step. Add code only when meaningful and tested. Retain important dates. Cite the actual original URL; do not hardcode an operator's domain or claim to have performed their experiments.

## Zenn draft

```yaml
---
title: "[natural Japanese title matching the actual claim]"
emoji: "📝"
type: "tech"
topics: []
published: false
---
```

Choose tech/idea to fit the content. Preserve meaning, uncertainty and attribution in Japanese; do not invent a self-introduction or experience. Verify the current platform's supported fields and limits before upload. Supply a plain-text draft if integration is unavailable.

## Review

Confirm sources, preferred original, privacy, author voice, target audience and draft status. Any schedule is a proposal. Publication requires authorization plus final readback.
