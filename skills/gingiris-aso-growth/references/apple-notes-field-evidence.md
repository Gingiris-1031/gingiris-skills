# Apple Notes field evidence — ASO / mobile growth

Source: Iris's Apple Notes, `ASO 学习 0307`, created and last modified 2026-03-07. This file is a privacy-safe synthesis, not a raw export.

## Directly supported operating observations

- Keyword research starts from user intent, then compares search demand, difficulty, competitor coverage and storefront/region differences.
- Astro was noted as a lightweight indie tool at roughly $80–90/year; 点点数据 was the domestic alternative mentioned.
- Listing creative should be treated as a conversion hypothesis and tested only after enough traffic exists.
- Mobile consumer products should test short-form video early; TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts have different audience and creative-fit patterns.
- The cold-start sequence recorded was: founder-operated organic content → identify a repeatable winning format → hand it to creators → amplify proven creative with paid distribution.
- Creator operations require contracts, payment, performance tracking and a manager/coach structure; past creator performance is not a guarantee of future performance.
- App onboarding and in-product conversion require finer instrumentation than a typical web funnel.

## Evidence boundary

The note is a learning-session record. It contains strategy observations and directional price references, but no controlled App Store before/after dataset. It does not establish causal uplift.

## Compliance boundary

The source note also records discussion of bought ratings, artificial search traffic, device/account farms and backup packages intended to evade enforcement. These are historical notes, not recommendations. This skill must never recommend or execute:

- fake reviews, bought ratings or incentivized reviews that violate store policy;
- artificial searches, installs, chart manipulation or coordinated device farms;
- identity, account, network or device tactics designed to evade platform review;
- cloned/backup packages used to bypass enforcement.

Use official Product Page Optimization / Store Listing Experiments, compliant review prompts, transparent paid acquisition and genuine-user research instead.
