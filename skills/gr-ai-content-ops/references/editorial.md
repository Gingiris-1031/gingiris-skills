# Source and editorial review

## Source record

Use `source_id`, source type, owner/speaker, date, exact URL/file/timecode/line, raw excerpt, corrected extract, claim type, verification status, audience permission, rights and expiry/check date. Keep private locators in the private work log, not a public deliverable.

- Direct data: retain the measured window, sampling/filtering method and denominator.
- Self-report: attribute it; do not convert a company's stated revenue or a founder's recollection into independently audited fact.
- Proposal: label planned, estimated or not yet executed; do not report forecast reach as observed reach.
- Interpretation: distinguish it from observation. Evidence may support several explanations.
- Missing evidence: list what could resolve it; do not fabricate a source or a measurement.

Preserve raw transcripts. Check numbers, names and speakers selectively where they matter; retain correction provenance. A quotation requires exact wording and attribution. A paraphrase must not be presented in quotation marks.

## Three-gate review record

| Gate | Review question | Result / change |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Are consequential facts supported and current? Are targets and company claims labeled? | Pass / revise / blocked, with locator |
| Voice | Does this fit authentic samples and the requested audience? Is any first-person experience invented? | Pass / revise, with example |
| Disclosure | Are consent, external rights and public scope clear? Can an anonymous story still identify someone? | Pass / revise / blocked |

Avoid mandatory artificial style rules. Remove filler where it obscures the argument. A sentence count, banned word or contrarian hook alone is not evidence of quality.

## Adaptation reconciliation

Create a parent-section → variant-section/card/timecode map. For each parent section record retained, intentionally summarized or intentionally omitted with reason. Compare coverage and key qualifiers; word count alone cannot establish semantic fidelity. Check visual overflow, cropped sentences, subtitle timing, links and media order. Preserve the approved exact wording when the user requires it.
