# Copyable operating templates

Populate only the requested scope. Blank and unknown are better than invented facts.

## 0. Adaptive intake

Ask only unanswered questions that affect this assignment. Example opening in Chinese:

> 为了让方案更贴近你的产品，先补充三点（已有的信息不用重复）：
> 1. 产品官网是什么？还没上线的话，一句话介绍产品也可以。
> 2. 想参考哪1–2个竞品？可以给网址，并说说想看早期冷启动还是现在的内容；不确定的话我可以先找候选。
> 3. 这次主要面向谁，最希望内容带来什么行动：了解产品、试用、预约演示，还是有效咨询？

English equivalent: “What is your product URL or short description? Which competitors should we learn from, and at what stage? Who should we reach and what action should the content drive?”

Follow up only as needed: target market/language; existing account URLs and metrics; public demos or approved case material; hours available per week; delivery dates. A supplied article rewrite usually needs only its audience, destination and intended action.

Use the [evidence-to-action card](competitor-content.md) for strategy and cold-start work. Add a one-line change log: previous plan → new plan → source → transfer constraint. Keep historical observations separate from proposed cadence and expected outcomes.
