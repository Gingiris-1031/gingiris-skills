# Channel decisions

Start from the audience's actual discovery and buying process. These options are experiments, not algorithm guarantees.

| Need | Candidate asset/channel | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Visible product effect | Short demonstration, visual before/after | Authentic output, context, rights and a working next step |
| Complex workflow | Tutorial, comparison, product/engineering article | Reader task, reproducible steps and current product behavior |
| Developer or procurement evaluation | Searchable docs, demo, use-case page, relevant professional channel | Qualified visits and evaluations, not only followers |
| Person-led trust | A supported judgment or real story | Authentic viewpoint, consent and audience fit |
| Community contribution | User story, learning assignment, AMA or editorial selection | Contributor permission, editorial quality and cohort retention |

Company account: explain capability, changes and proof. Founder/employee account: contextual judgment and real experience. These can differ without inventing personas.

## Adaptation

- X/short social: give one useful point and its support. Test thread length, link placement and cadence for this account; do not assert a universal link penalty or mandatory two-post limit.
- WeChat/long-form: structure around the reader's task. Series and internal links may help navigation; neither 2,000 words nor a fixed promotional footer is a requirement. Validate the editor's current limits and preview.
- Xiaohongshu/cards: choose a searchable problem, legible cover and one idea per card. A 1080×1440 canvas is a design starting point, not a platform requirement. Match source sections to cards; extra pages or explicit edits are preferable to silent truncation.
- Video: identify the useful moment before polishing. Record source timecodes, speaker consent, subtitle accuracy and per-platform cover/metadata needs. Inspect the current editor rather than copying historical DOM selectors.
- Community/newsletter: prepare a context-specific introduction and useful next action. Contributor notification and group posting need authorization.

## Reuse map

A reviewed tutorial might become a short viewpoint, a card set and a demonstration script. Each variant has its own hook, length, evidence mapping, next action and delivery state. Seven formats are a possibility, not a quota. Reuse can span weeks if the claim is still current and the audience/context is distinct.

Keep campaign messaging consistent between social and the relevant landing page. Changing the website requires scope and authorization; do not update it merely because a content plan mentions alignment.
