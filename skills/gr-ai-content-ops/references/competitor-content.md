# Competitor content research / 竞品内容研究

## Scope before search

Choose 1–2 comparators by buyer, job and business model; a user-named competitor is a starting point, not proof of identical positioning. Define the decision the research must change. For cold start, anchor a dated public launch and a bounded period (for example the next 30–90 days). Call it an observed launch window, not the first-ever activity unless established. Date of retrieval and date of publication are separate.

## Reconstruct assets and their path to action

Prefer contemporaneous founder/company posts, launch threads, dated release notes, videos, documentation/repository history and archived pages. Read comments where they reveal actual questions or adoption friction. Search coverage is a sample; missing search results do not prove a channel was unused. Modern navigation and updated pages must not be attributed to their older article date. Current repository HEAD is not a historical README.

Record for each useful asset: date and locator; target buyer; problem/hook; format and demonstration; next action and friction; founder/company/community roles; response evidence; uncertainty. Check whether a linked demo/tutorial is merely referenced or actually accessible/tested. Do not run paid calls or contact people just to audit content.

Distinguish observed publication, company self-report, audience comment and analyst inference. Current likes/followers are not launch-day metrics. Keep paid/organic mix and conversion unknown unless sourced. Attention does not establish revenue causation. Include negative feedback, not only successful assets. Research methods and original paraphrases; do not copy competitor wording, testimonials or media.

## Transfer methods, not advantages

For each tactic assess buyer fit, product readiness, founder reach, partner/investor amplification, budget and rights. Mark adopt, adapt, defer or reject, with a reason. A developer API launch and an operator-facing service may share a demo-first method but require different channels and explanation. Investor promotion is a resource dependency, not a universal playbook step.

Convert findings into a parent asset, channel role, CTA, owner, time estimate, readiness gate and review metric. When proof/demo is unavailable, plan its production and hold claims until verified. Use a clearly labeled storyboard or requirements discussion as a fallback; never present it as a tested product or customer result. Do not default to high-volume SEO because a mature competitor has a large blog today.
