# Website to social / 官网内容与社媒联动

## Decide the order

Use the intake and competitor evidence to identify the bottleneck. Searchable buyer questions plus a useful product next step justify a durable guide or comparison page. A product whose effect must be heard or seen may need a real demonstration first. A team exploring positioning may test a supported viewpoint on social before building a long article. Search discovery and social feedback can run concurrently; do not wait for rankings to distribute a ready article.

Inventory equivalent existing URLs before creating new ones. Record reader question, intent, useful evidence, conflicting claims, CTA, freshness and available performance. Choose update/create/consolidate/defer. Missing analytics means priorities are hypotheses, not proven traffic opportunities. Product competitors and search/content competitors may differ.

## Review the parent

Owned pages are sources to inspect, not ground truth. Check dated numbers, personal experiences, customer stories, superlatives, pricing and signup conditions. Contradictory copy is a verification task, not permission to choose the more attractive promise. Do not repeat unsupported claims merely with a website citation. Preserve useful sections while correcting or excluding unsupported material within authorized scope.

Answer the buyer question directly; include meaningful examples, evidence dates, relevant product links and a next step. Use diagrams/tables when they improve comprehension. Label illustrative material. Do not invent research, keyword volumes, benchmarks or cases to fill a format. Technical eligibility, indexing, rankings, AI citations and conversions are different outcomes. Structured data is not a guarantee of AI citation. SEO/GEO is not a reason to generate repetitive question pages.

## LinkedIn adaptation

Give the post a specific scenario, supported insight, reasoning and useful takeaway. The reader should benefit without clicking. Add the relevant blog/report URL where it supplies deeper detail, rather than replacing the post with a teaser. Link placement is an account-level experiment, not a universal algorithm rule.

Choose a visual by the job: comparison table for a choice, flow diagram for responsibility, document carousel for steps, dated report excerpt for evidence, real video for behavior. Text-only remains valid for a substantive argument. Do not add decorative images to disguise thin copy. Specify visual content, source, rights, caption/alt text and readiness. A brief or storyboard is not a produced visual.

Keep reviewer instructions and uncertainty checks in a separate production note. Public copy should retain material limitations naturally, without internal phrases like 'not a reported test result' unless genuinely needed to prevent misunderstanding.

## Link and measurement gates

Before publishing a linked variant, verify the article's actual public URL, correct language, mobile readability, title/share preview and matching CTA. If unpublished, keep the link pending; never send readers to an invented slug. Native standalone variants can be ready independently. Use consistent UTM parameters for external campaign links, not internal site navigation. Record campaign/content IDs and the destination URL.

Measure three paths separately: search impressions/clicks; social impressions/outbound visits; meaningful product events. Define the activation event and denominator before interpreting it. Report request is not successful generation; lead form view is not qualified inquiry; AI citation is not conversion. Attribution depends on available instrumentation/consent. No access means unknown. Select a small pilot that fits actual writing, fact-checking, design and review hours, reducing cadence when proof production takes longer.

## Depth and cooperative verification

User-confirmed first-party cases can anchor a substantive draft. Verify specific consequential claims without dismissing the entire source. A matching local source and public page confirms consistency, not independent measurement. Keep dates/estimate definitions beside numbers; put unresolved source questions in production notes. Expand with a worked decision, alternatives, reasoning and an actionable next step. Avoid short generic seeds as final deliverables.

For search-oriented parent articles, complete the [keyword and positioning brief](search-positioning.md) before writing. The social hook may differ from the search title while preserving the article’s promise.
