# Design provenance

Version 1.0.0 adapts the author-provided Chinese guide “Gingiris AI 内容运营指南”, revision 65, reviewed on 2026-11-12. The private source export is not included or required to use this skill.

Retained mechanisms: three source lines; audience-stage and channel roles; raw-material provenance; weekly capacity planning; evidence/voice/disclosure gates; parent-to-variant reuse; explicit delivery receipts; contributor distribution handoff; and content/account/business measurement.

Generalization decisions:
- Internal prices, staffing capacity, account analytics and customer anecdotes were not republished as benchmarks.
- Named-company revenue, growth timelines and social metrics were omitted because this release did not independently reproduce their primary evidence.
- Posting ratios, frequencies, lengths and formatting settings are configurable hypotheses, not universal performance rules.
- Historical platform limits and browser selectors were replaced with current-editor/documentation checks.
- Anonymous customer stories and exact quotations were excluded; removing names alone can leave identity recoverable.
- Automatic catch-up and contributor announcements became bounded recovery and authorized handoff steps.
- First-person style, title correlations and outlier ratios do not establish causal recommendations.

The skill is a reusable operating method, not a republication of the guide or a claim that its historical outcomes will repeat.
