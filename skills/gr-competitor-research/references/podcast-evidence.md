# Podcast evidence for competitor research

1. Use star-history/OSS Insight to locate a launch inflection date.
2. Restrict search to that date/week and reconstruct PR, directories, communities and creator amplification.
3. Compare first site, beta, first launch and current site.
4. Benchmark the strongest operator per channel, not one competitor everywhere.
5. Treat a competitor funding announcement as a category-attention hypothesis, not proof that copying will work.
