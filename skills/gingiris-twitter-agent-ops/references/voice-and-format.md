# Voice versus performance / 文风与分发效果

When using best/worst posts, record the metric and whether “best” means the author's preference or audience response. Compare topic, language, format, timeliness, audience size, post age and promotion before attributing performance to style. Few contrasting samples start hypotheses, not permanent bans.

Rule card: observed pattern → original examples and counterexample → author preference or performance hypothesis → scope → confidence → future sample/review condition.

Constructed example: recent Chinese product experiments outperform older English aphorisms. This does not isolate language: specificity, topic, timing and audience also differ. Retain “test concrete original experience” as a candidate; do not ban English unless the author chooses that preference. No fixed word count, first-person opening or mandatory number follows from engagement alone.

Hold out samples or compare future drafts for meaning and voice. Keep distribution tests separate from author approval. Another person's experience remains attributed, even when adapting their format; never write it as the user's first-person achievement.


## Format decision / 格式选择

Use the user's requested format first. Otherwise select a single post or a thread from the source's structure, audience, account evidence and verified publisher capabilities. Do not default to 10–15 posts or reject a requested thread because a different account historically performed poorly. Record format choice as a testable decision. A draft request does not schedule or publish it.
