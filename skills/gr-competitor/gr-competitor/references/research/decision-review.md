# Decision review / 从研究到决定

Use when a teardown must inform a product, positioning or channel decision. The examples below are constructed teaching examples, not customer results.

## Match the page to the visitor's task / 先识别页面任务

| Page | Visitor's question | Inspect | Candidate outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing landing page / 营销官网 | Is this for me, why trust it? | Target user, task, evidence, eligibility, next step | Qualified trial or demo |
| Content or project community / 素材社区 | Can I find and use the right project? | Search, previews, usable files, requirements, first-use path | Relevant project used or completed |
| Tool workspace / 工具工作台 | How do I finish my task? | Inputs, permissions, defaults, error recovery | Task completion without assistance |

Record acquisition source, existing product knowledge and first value action. Do not compare screen counts across different page purposes or require every community to become a marketing landing page. For a two-sided community, separate creators/suppliers from consumers; existing hardware owners may have different entry needs. A reported conversion drop after a redesign is a hypothesis until cohort, traffic mix and instrumentation are checked.

For products with eligibility constraints, compare supported region/entity, goods or task type, onboarding/approval, buyer, implementation and service dependencies. Mark unsupported eligibility or payment claims unknown until verified from an authoritative source. Price and feature lists alone cannot establish usability.

## Social evidence worksheet / 社媒数据口径

For each dataset record date/timezone, historical vs current stage, account role, sampling/full enumeration, window, missing pages, original/quote/repost/reply inclusion and collection timestamp.

Keep interaction numerator and denominator explicit: (named reactions + comments + shares) / impressions is different from / followers. Current followers are not historical followers. Do not mix datasets with different inclusion rules; show sample size and median alongside a mean when outliers dominate. Public views, site visits, unique visitors, registrations and payments are distinct measures. Separate known paid/organic data; unknown promotion is not zero paid spend.

| Observation | Unsupported leap | Useful next check |
|---|---|---|
| Direct is the largest traffic category | Word of mouth caused growth | Attribution gaps, returning users, campaign and referral evidence |
| One channel paused posting | The whole company stopped marketing | Other channels and creator collaborations in the same window |
| Several posts share a hook | They were all paid or fake | Disclosure, account roles and corroborating evidence; otherwise unknown |
| Large views, few interactions | Bots or wasted budget | Distribution and denominator, qualified visits and downstream behavior |
| A launch and traffic peak coincide | Launch caused all growth | Dates, other events, source-level traffic; retain alternative explanations |

Comments enter the needs worksheet when they describe a task, obstacle, alternative or concrete usage. Generic praise or restated copy is low-information, not proof of a bot. A complaint does not establish willingness to switch or pay.

## One-page decision card / 一页决策卡

- Decision and relevant stage:
- Observation + original URL/date + sampling limit:
- Interpretation and plausible alternative explanation:
- Why it applies to our user/task; why it may not:
- Proposed change, owner and smallest test:
- Time/cash cap, metric denominator/window, continue/change/stop condition:
- What remains unverified:

Before accepting an AI-generated conclusion, answer: where is the source; why does it change our decision; what would overturn it? If the author cannot explain a claim, retrieve evidence, narrow it or mark it unknown. Fluent presentation is not verification. Do not infer a cold-start plan solely from a mature company's current channels.

Constructed example: a file-library homepage emphasizes company positioning while visitors seek a compatible template. Propose a project-first entry for that cohort; measure successful file use as well as signup. Do not promise a conversion lift or assume all traffic has the same intent.
