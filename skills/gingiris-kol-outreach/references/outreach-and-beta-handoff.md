# Outreach measurement and beta handoff

Use when a creator campaign also recruits product testers. Decide whether the primary goal is awareness, fundraising communication or product learning; do not use one goal's metrics as proof of another.

## Compare channels with observed stages

`Channel | Audience and sample | Contact date | Delivered / reached | Connection accepted | Substantive reply | Agreed next action | Observation window`

Keep connection acceptance separate from a reply. Use comparable audiences and windows before preferring a channel. Historical reply-rate anecdotes are not universal baselines. Follow-up should be relevant and bounded by recipient preference and platform rules.

## Hand interest over to a test owner

`Creator | Expressed interest | Invitation sent | Actual first use | Test task completed | Feedback received | Owner / next action / date`

Agree the beta entry date, capacity, task and learning objective with the product owner before promising access. A positive reply is not activation; an invitation is not usage. Track blockers separately, and carry only consented contact information into the test process. This card complements the campaign's existing attribution and paid-partnership disclosures.
