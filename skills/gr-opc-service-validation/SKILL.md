# OPC Service Validation / OPC 服务验证

## Scope / 范围

Validate one professional service against real buyer behavior and sustainable delivery. This is the focused experiment stage; `gingiris-solopreneur-coach` covers broader direction-finding and coaching. Neither is required to install the other. Do not rename the user's business into consulting if they chose a product; test the relevant assumption or explain that another workflow fits better.

本 Skill 聚焦一个专业服务的真实需求与可持续交付验证；已有 `gingiris-solopreneur-coach` 负责更广的方向探索与陪跑。两者可独立使用。不把用户选择的产品业务强改为咨询，也不要求辞职、注册公司或先免费劳动。

Use known context, ask only the missing decision-critical question, then produce a usable draft. Follow the user's language; bilingual references are available, but do not duplicate every answer unless requested.

使用已有背景，只补影响决定的关键信息，然后产出草稿。跟随用户语言；不默认每次双语输出。

## Workflow / 流程

1. **Evidence of ability / 能力证据**：列出实际完成的任务、本人贡献、可展示产物、结果口径和可使用权限。区分平台资源与个人能力。能力标签或过往职位本身不证明可交付。List completed work, personal contribution, artifacts and permission to show it; distinguish personal ability from platform advantages.
2. **One bounded offer / 一个有边界的服务**：指定买方、任务、交付物、排除项、所需客户输入、时间与验收。避免“什么都能帮”。Name the buyer, task, deliverable, exclusions, required input, timeline and acceptance criteria.
3. **Fit before work / 先筛适配**：确认问题优先级、决策者、预算/付款意愿、执行配合与数据可得性。顾问、创始人与内部执行者分别承担什么？Qualify urgency, decision authority, willingness to pay, access and execution ownership; avoid solving an unowned problem.
4. **Bounded showcase or paid pilot / 有上限的样例或付费试单**：读取 [试单模板](references/pilot.md)。声明教学样例、真实项目和已验收案例的区别；免费/折价仅是可选实验，写工时/支出上限，不承诺收入。Read the pilot worksheet; label demonstrations versus accepted client work. Free work is optional, capped and never required.
5. **Pricing and capacity / 定价与容量**：比较同范围报价、交付成本、返工与沟通负担、客户接受度和个人可持续程度。写清币种、税费/费用口径、付款节点和范围变更方式；具体法律条款需另按需求核验。Compare like-for-like scope and full effort, not a celebrity consultant's rate. Define scope changes and payment milestones.
6. **Accept, settle, review / 验收、回款、复盘**：交付完成、客户验收、实际到账、复购/转介绍分开记录。读取 [复盘与容量表](references/review.md)，给继续/修改/暂停结论及改变结论的证据。Delivery, acceptance, payment and repeat demand are separate observations.

## Decision discipline / 判断纪律

Warm feedback is not payment evidence. One successful delivery does not prove PMF or recurring revenue. Track the next similar request, scope stability, repeat purchase and marginal effort. A narrow service with healthy capacity can be a valid destination; hiring or platform-building is optional.

称赞不是付款；一单成功不等于 PMF、订阅收入或可复制商业模式。观察相似需求、范围稳定性、复购与新增交付成本。保持小规模也是有效选择，团队化与平台化并非必经终点。

A useful finish is a filled pilot card, one next action, an observation window and a stopping condition. Lack of evidence is a reason to reduce uncertainty, not invent conversion rates. Income targets and workload limits are personal inputs, not universal thresholds.

完成标准是可填写并执行的试单卡、下一步动作、观察窗口与停止条件。收入目标与工时上限来自个人条件，不套用“赚5000即可辞职”等阈值。联系客户、签约、收款、公开案例均按用户实际授权执行。
